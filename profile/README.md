# Kinprove

**Let evidence reveal biological connections.**

A genealogy research platform that brings DNA matches and family trees together to help you investigate biological relationships.

[Explore Kinprove](https://kinprove.io) · [Get help](https://kinprove.io/support)

## Research tools

- **[Genealogy Research Recipes](https://github.com/Kinprove/genealogy-research-recipes)** — Source-backed genealogy research workflows for Claude, Hermes Agent and OpenClaw. No Kinprove account required.
- **[Kinprove Genealogist](https://github.com/Kinprove/claude-skill-kinprove-genealogist/tree/main/kinprove-genealogist)** — A Claude skill for DNA matches, family hypotheses and Kinprove projects, with optional access to your data through MCP.
